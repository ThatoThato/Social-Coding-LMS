# Data & Spreadsheets — Worksheet

## Task 1: Sorting and filtering
I sorted the learner list by surname and then filtered to Grade 11 only.
The filter left 14 rows out of the original 38.

## Task 2: Formulas
- Total: `=SUM(C2:C39)`
- Average: `=AVERAGE(C2:C39)`
- Pass count: `=COUNTIF(C2:C39,">=50")`

The average came to 61.4 and 27 of the 38 learners passed.

## Task 3: Chart
I used a bar chart because the data compares separate groups rather than
showing change over time. A line chart would have implied a trend between
categories that does not exist.

## What I found difficult
Getting COUNTIF to accept the ">=50" as text in quotes. I first wrote it
without the quotes and it returned an error.
