Digital Literacy & Cyber Safety - Answers

1. A message says I have won data and asks me to click a link.
   What do I check first?
   I check who sent it. If it is a number I do not know, or the link does not
   match the real company name, I do not click it. I would go to the company's
   real website myself instead of using the link.

2. A friend asks for my password "just to check something".
   I would say no. Even if I trust the friend, once someone else has my
   password I cannot control who else gets it. If they need something from my
   account I can open it myself while they watch.

3. Which of my accounts share one password?
   My email and my school login use the same password. I am going to change
   the school one this week so they are different, because if one leaks the
   other is exposed too.
